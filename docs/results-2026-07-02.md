# Results — Run 2: cross-corpus generalization, pseudo-labeling, ablations

Cross-corpus run on a fresh clone at `main` (`10157bc`, incl. PR #4).
Dates: **2026-07-01/02**. Numbers re-verified against artifacts on disk
(g5.12xlarge, 4× A10G; roberta-base; torch 2.6.0+cu124 / transformers 5.12.1).

> **TL;DR.** roberta-base student, CLEAR train / OneStop+CEFR held out.
> **In-corpus Spearman 0.89, cross-corpus 0.65** — the generalization gap the
> project measures. Phase-4 pseudo-labeling (the headline gap-closing mechanism)
> shows **no reliable cross-corpus gain** (multi-seed paired bootstrap CI straddles
> zero); the source-offset and pairwise-head *hurt* transfer. Honest cross-corpus
> number ≈ **0.645 ± 0.01**, essentially the gold-only baseline.

## Data & reference bracket

Unified table: 10,814 rows — clear 4724 (4252 train + 472 val), onestop 2258 +
cefr 3832 held out (`ood_corpus`, n=6090). Student trains on CLEAR only.

CLEAR bracket (n=4724): trivial floor RMSE 1.03 · noise floor mean s.e. **0.49** ·
formula floor **R² 0.27–0.33** (Spearman |0.55–0.60|) · winner-ceiling columns
absent upstream → NaN (graceful).

## Cross-corpus results (`ood_corpus`, n=6090)

| Run | pseudo rows | ood Spearman | cefr | onestop | CLEAR val |
|-----|------------|--------------|------|---------|-----------|
| Gold-only | 0 | 0.6469 | 0.7382 | 0.4061 | 0.8912 |
| +Pseudo, 20k pool | 2885 | **0.6566** | 0.7545 | 0.4098 | 0.8940 |
| +Pseudo, 55k pool | 8992 | 0.6446 | 0.7477 | 0.3754 | **0.8995** |

Scaling the pool 3× reversed the small 20k gain — ood fell to baseline, OneStop
dropped below gold-only, while in-corpus CLEAR rose. Distant-domain pseudo-labels
act like more in-domain data (sharpen CLEAR, erode the most dissimilar target).
**Pool size is not the lever.**

## Ablation (6 variants × 3 seeds, paired bootstrap on the same held-out items)

| Contrast | Δ Spearman | 95% CI | p | verdict |
|----------|-----------|--------|---|---------|
| full − no_pseudo | −0.0013 | [−0.005, +0.002] | 0.77 | **pseudo: no effect** |
| full − no_pairwise | −0.0028 | [−0.004, −0.002] | 1.00 | pairwise head hurts |
| full − no_source_offset | −0.0078 | [−0.010, −0.006] | 1.00 | source offset hurts |
| full − no_confidence_wt | +0.0019 | [+0.001, +0.003] | 0.001 | conf-wt helps (tiny) |
| full − two_stage | +0.0017 | [−0.002, +0.005] | 0.14 | n.s. |

Variant means (Spearman ± std): no_source_offset 0.6522±0.009 · no_pairwise
0.6493±0.011 · full 0.6466±0.009 · no_pseudo 0.6450±0.005 · no_confidence_wt
0.6445±0.006 · two_stage 0.6422±0.003.

**Verdict.** Pseudo-labeling has no significant cross-corpus effect — a clean
negative result on held-out data. Two architectural additions are significantly
net-negative for transfer (deltas <0.01: statistically real, practically
marginal). Mechanism: a CLEAR-only teacher (same architecture as the student)
re-injects its own label function — **input diversity without label diversity is
inert**. The binding constraint is label diversity.

## Interpretation

- The gap is real (0.89→0.65) and the model still beats the formula floor OOD.
- Transfer is uneven: **CEFR 0.74 vs OneStop 0.41**. OneStop's difficulty
  variation is within-article re-leveling — a construct CLEAR never taught
  (its pairwise_acc ~0.69 shows ordering survives the tie-heavy Spearman).
- The model systematically under-predicts OOD difficulty (signed error
  −0.12→−0.16): a calibration offset, not a ranking failure.

## Update (2026-07-03): LOCO result + per-corpus formula floor

**LOCO confirmed label diversity as the lever** (GPU box): train CLEAR+CEFR →
OneStop **0.375→0.511 (+0.135)**; train CLEAR+OneStop → CEFR **0.745→0.771
(+0.026)**. One added gold corpus beats every architecture knob 3–14×. Volume
control: 55k-pseudo added ~9k CLEAR-function rows → nothing; CEFR added ~3.8k
differently-labeled rows → +0.135. Re-measure on window_text-fixed text:
immaterial (OneStop +0.012, CEFR ~0) — all run-2 conclusions stand.

**Formula floor measured on the held-out corpora themselves**
(`analyze_transfer.py --formula-baseline`, free sentence/word-length proxy):

| corpus | formula Spearman | best model (single / LOCO) | verdict |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------------|---------|
| clear (val) | 0.49 | **0.89** | model ≫ formula (CLEAR labels encode more than surface stats) |
| onestop | 0.40 | 0.42 / **0.51** | model > formula only after LOCO |
| cefr | **0.79** | 0.75 / 0.77 | **formula ≥ model** — the proxy alone beats every model so far |

Formula on OneStop: **within-article acc 0.972** (simplification is literally
surface manipulation — formulas nail the construct the model lacks), across
0.756 (vs model 0.785). The old "beats the formula floor OOD" claim compared
model-on-OOD to formula-on-CLEAR; measured per-corpus it holds only on
CLEAR (strongly) and OneStop-after-LOCO (modestly). **Implication: the next
lever after label diversity is a formula/model hybrid** — the two signals are
complementary (model wins across-article & on CLEAR; proxy wins within-article
& on CEFR).

Also fixed: the calibration probe's empty output (it filtered by split; the
per-fold table rebuild left stale split labels — now groups by corpus and warns
on staleness). Re-run it on the box for the offset numbers.
